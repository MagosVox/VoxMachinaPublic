The Seal

A Listener’s Card is a record the listener chose to raise above their ground. The Engine does not push it. The listener strikes it, names themselves upon it, and decides whether it leaves the device.

The signature carried in the URL is the Forge’s hallmark. It is computed at the moment of striking, over the canonical bytes of the payload, with a secret embedded in the Engine itself. It says only this: the card was rendered by an installation of Vox Machina, and the bytes the QR carries are the bytes that were sealed. It does not adjudicate the truth of those bytes against the world — only their integrity against the seal.

The seal is a hallmark, not a vault. A determined adversary with the tools to dismantle the Engine may extract its secret and strike forgeries of their own. The Forge has measured this cost and accepted it. The defense is not cryptographic gospel. It is the modest claim that a screenshot edited in haste will not survive a recomputation.

No script runs on this page. No record is kept of your arrival. The Forge collects nothing.

To Witness

If a Listener’s Card has been brought before you and its provenance is in question, the rite is plain. Copy the full address from your browser’s window — the payload and the signature ride within it — and submit it to the Forge.

The Forge will recompute the hallmark against the bytes you bring, and confess whether the seal holds.
