# Vox Machina Audio — Data Sanctity Covenant

**Effective date:** 2026-03-28

By the Omnissiah's decree, this application honours the sanctity of your data.

Vox Machina does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All signal processing is performed locally upon your device — your machine-spirit alone bears witness to the audio it renders. No telemetry, no analytics, no data-tithes are extracted.

No network connections are established. No user accounts are required. No information leaves the sacred boundary of your hardware.

The application requires USB host permission to communicate with USB audio devices. This permission is used exclusively for isochronous audio output and is never used to access data on connected devices.

Your data is your own. The Forge demands nothing of it.
