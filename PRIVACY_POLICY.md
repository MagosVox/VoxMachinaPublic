# Vox Machina Audio — Data Sanctity Covenant

**Effective date:** 2026-03-28

By the Forge's decree, this application honours the sanctity of your data.

Vox Machina does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to its developer. All signal processing is performed locally upon your device — your machine alone bears witness to the audio it renders. No telemetry, no analytics, no data-tithes are extracted.

No information is transmitted to the developer. No user accounts are required. The Forge demands nothing of your data.

Scrobbling (optional): If you choose to connect a Last.fm or ListenBrainz account, the application will transmit track metadata (title, artist, album) to that service as you listen. This feature is disabled by default and requires explicit configuration. Pending submissions are queued locally on your device until delivered. All data handling by these services is governed by their respective privacy policies. Vox Machina receives none of this data.

The application requires USB host permission to communicate with USB audio devices. This permission is used exclusively for isochronous audio output and is never used to access data on connected devices.
